# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Assume the other person is trying to help. Talk about the work, not
the person doing it.

## What is expected

- Take other people seriously, including the ones who are new to this.
- Say what you mean plainly, and let other people do the same.
- Accept that a decision may go against you, and say why you disagree without
  making it personal.
- Correct a mistake and move on. Yours or someone else's.

## What is not

- Insults, or comments about who someone is rather than what they wrote.
- Sexual language or attention in any project space.
- Harassment, in the open or in private. Following someone from thread to
  thread counts.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Repeatedly pushing past a "no".

## Where this applies

Every space this project uses: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Mail **admin@dakroub.co**. Say what happened and where. You do not need to
have all the details, and you do not need to be the person it happened to.

Reports stay private. The person who reports is never named to the person
reported without asking first.

## What happens next

A report gets a reply. Depending on what happened, the answer is a private
word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a permanent one. Whoever
handles a report steps aside if it involves them.

Enforcement is by the maintainers. Today that is Joe Dakroub.
