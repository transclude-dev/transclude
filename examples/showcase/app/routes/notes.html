Nothing here needs JavaScript to work. The form submits, POST runs, and the loader renders what it left behind. The oldest way to change state on the web, and still the one that works everywhere.

Every mutation ends in a 303 redirect, so the URL you land on is a GET: refreshing repeats that, not the submission. A rejected note is the exception. Nothing changed, so it comes straight back with the reason.

The list below is a fragment: it renders here as part of the document and answers on its own at /notes?fragment=notes, from the same compiled markup. Swapping it in without a reload is a client's job, and this framework does not ship one. Bring htmx, Turbo, or ten lines of fetch. What arrives will define and style itself either way.
