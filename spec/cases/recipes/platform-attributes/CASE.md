The docs dialog, accordion and findable text are the platform's own
attributes. The compiler passes `command`, `commandfor`, a shared `name` on
`<details>` and `hidden="until-found"` through as written, and ships no
JavaScript for them.

Source: `/docs/recipes`, "Open a dialog with no JavaScript", "An accordion
where one is open at a time" and "Collapsed content that a page search can
still find".
